DASHBOARD IDEAS
In OrderDepth we never see the takers as they never end up in the OB. We only see the makers. The trades data is used for
the takers. Basic intuition:

1. Triangle (Taker) at the Ask: Someone just "lifted the offer." They bought aggressively.
2. Triangle (Taker) at the Bid: Someone just "hit the bid." They sold aggressively.
3. Square (Maker) Trade: This usually shows up when a large Taker order eats through multiple levels of the book, hitting several "resting" Maker orders in a row.

So many clustered squares means some taker (whale) is aggressively eating the liquidity and if there are many triangles
then there is and aggressive demand increase. 

We plot ask quotes in red, bid quotes in blue for clear distinction. We do NOT want data smoothing, instead we want 
a plot option where the line only changes when the numerical value actually changes. We then plot our own potential trades as crosses.
