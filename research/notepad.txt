IMPORTAN EXCHANGE MECHANICS
- orders are IOC (Immediate or Cancel) and not Fill or Kill. That means that if an order is placed
  for 100 units bid at 100 and only 40 exist at that ask then the order is partially filled and the rest 
  is cancelled. 

- Prosperity is a "Turn-Based" simulation disguised as a continuous market.
    1. Start of Tick: Everyone (Bots and You) submits orders simultaneously.
    2. Matching Phase: The engine matches all these orders against each other and the existing book.
    3. Snapshot Phase: The engine looks at the result of all those matches and writes the "Activities" log (your pdf).

End of Tick: The round is over.
