IMPORTAN EXCHANGE MECHANICS
- orders are IOC (Immediate or Cancel) and not Fill or Kill. That means that if an order is placed
  for 100 units bid at 100 and only 40 exist at that ask then the order is partially filled and the rest 
  is cancelled. 

- Prosperity is a "Turn-Based" simulation disguised as a continuous market.
    1. Start of Tick: Everyone (Bots and You) submits orders simultaneously.
    2. Matching Phase: The engine matches all these orders against each other and the existing book.
    3. Snapshot Phase: The engine looks at the result of all those matches and writes the "Activities" log (your pdf).

End of Tick: The round is over.

OBSERVATIONS & CONVERSIONS 
- Observations are extra intel we get about products, aka external data
- Conversions allows us to at the end of a round send how many of our current products we want to 
  "convert" aka sell or buy with another island. This is essentially arb markets BUT they come 
  with costs suchs as exportFees etc. So basically:
    1. Round takes place
    2. We get observations and decide if a conversion is possible
    3. If profitable we convert our position with another island, the important part
    is that this allows us to capture arbitrage and make trades even if the orderbook
    in our home market lacks liquidity

ONLY ONE product in the simulation at a time is allowed for conversions. Aka we only need to return an INT:
- conversions = 10 (we buy 10 units from another island)
- conversions = -10 (we sell 10 units to another island)
